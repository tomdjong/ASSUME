ASSUME

About ASSUME

logo by Jacob Neumann

ASSUME is an informal research level seminar on univalent mathematics between the universities of Birmingham and Nottingham.

The seminar provides a platform for discussing recent work where we try to make progress on open problems. It also offers opportunities for PhD students to get acquainted with the current interests of members of the community.

Organizers:
  • Eric Finster (University of Birmingham)
  • Tom de Jong (University of Nottingham)
